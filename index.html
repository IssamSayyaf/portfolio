Issam Sayyaf

Deep learning for time-series sensor data — Senior Algorithm Engineer, Edge AI at TDK, Grenoble

I build deep-learning models for noisy, multi-modal sensor time series. As a Senior Algorithm Engineer on the Edge AI team at TDK InvenSense in Grenoble, I train CNNs, RNNs/LSTMs and Transformers for forecasting, anomaly detection, gesture recognition and spatial tracking — then get them running on-device, quantized and pruned, through ONNX and TensorFlow Lite.

My PhD (Université Gustave Eiffel, 2026) was on physics-informed anomaly detection for navigation time series, and I surveyed the field for IEEE Sensors Journal in 2025. Before that I spent several years building production Yocto systems and Linux kernel drivers — which is why the models I ship tend to survive contact with real hardware.

I write about this occasionally. The fastest way to reach me is email.

What I do

Time-Series Deep Learning

CNNs, RNNs/LSTMs and Transformers for forecasting, anomaly detection, gesture recognition and spatial tracking on sensor streams. PyTorch and TensorFlow.

Transformers & Sequence Models

Time-series Transformers, behavioural embeddings and tokenized sensor streams — adapting sequence architectures, including LLM-style ones, to raw telemetry.

Anomaly Detection

Reconstruction-based methods — autoencoders (AE, VAE), adversarial generative models, isolation forests — plus the evaluation methodology: thresholds, metrics and what they hide.

DSP & Feature Engineering

Filtering, windowing, FFT/PSD and spectral analysis to clean and calibrate noisy sensor data, and engineer features robust enough to learn from.

Edge & Embedded Optimisation

Quantization, pruning and hardware-accelerated compilation (ONNX, TensorRT, TensorFlow Lite) for deployment on resource-constrained devices.

ML Pipelines & Data

Pipelines for high-volume streaming, asynchronous telemetry at low latency — plus the data-collection protocols and sensor test benches that feed them.

Sensor Characterisation

Experimental evaluation across environments: microphone, accelerometer, gyroscope, ultrasonic time-of-flight, pressure and magnetometer. NI DAQ, LabVIEW, SCPI benches.

Embedded Systems

5+ years of C/C++ on ARM Cortex-M and -A: Zephyr, FreeRTOS, Yocto BSPs, Linux kernel drivers, secure boot and OTA, SPI/I2C/UART/CAN, BLE, GNSS.

Selected projects

Anomaly Detection on IMU Time Series

Physics-informed AE/VAE/USAD models catching sensor faults in real time on embedded devices.

5G LOS/NLOS Detection with USAD

Unsupervised anomaly detection on 5G channel impulse responses, separating line-of-sight from NLOS.

STM32MP157 Yocto BSP
